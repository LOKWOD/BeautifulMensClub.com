05 / FIELD NOTES

Small moves.
Visible results.

The stuff that sounds minor until every minor thing is handled.

2 MINUTES

Steam the collar.

A good shirt with a collapsed collar still looks tired. Steam it, shape it and let it cool before putting it on.

5 MINUTES

Clear the bathroom counter.

Keep daily products on one tray. Everything else belongs in a drawer. You just made the whole room feel better.

10 MINUTES

Walk before the call.

Movement fixes posture, breathing and energy faster than another cup of coffee. Take the call standing if you can.
