05 / FIELD NOTES

Small moves.
Visible results.

The things that sound minor until every minor thing is handled.

2 MINUTES

Steam the collar.

A good shirt with a collapsed collar still looks tired. Shape it and let it cool before putting it on.

5 MINUTES

Clear the counter.

Keep daily products on one tray. Everything else belongs in a drawer. The room instantly feels better.

10 MINUTES

Walk before the call.

Movement improves posture, breathing and energy faster than another cup of coffee.
