PRIVACY · LAST UPDATED AUGUST 14, 2026

Plain language.
No mystery box.

Beautiful Men's Club is currently a static publication. This page explains what the site stores on your device, what happens when you send an email and what could change as the club grows.

The practical summary: the site does not currently operate user accounts, run its own advertising tracker or accept payment. The Sunday Reset saves checklist progress in your browser. Joining the founding list opens your email application so you can review and send the message yourself.

1. Information you choose to send

When you email hello@beautifulmensclub.com, the publication receives the information included in that message, such as your name, email address, feedback or founding-reader request. That information may be used to reply, maintain the founding contact list, improve the publication or handle a correction.

The current join form does not silently submit information to a web database. It assembles a message and opens your device's email application. You can review, edit or abandon the message before sending it.

2. Information stored on your device

The interactive Sunday Reset uses browser local storage to remember which checklist items you completed. This data remains in that browser on that device unless you clear the checklist, clear browser storage or use a browser mode that removes local data.

The saved checklist contains task identifiers such as “outfits,” “workouts” and “calendar.” It is not transmitted to Beautiful Men's Club by the site's current code.

3. Hosting and basic technical records

The site is published through GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, the hosting infrastructure may process ordinary technical information needed to deliver pages securely and reliably, which can include IP address, request time, requested page and browser information. Beautiful Men's Club does not currently receive a custom visitor-level analytics dashboard from the site's own code.

4. Fonts and third-party requests

The site currently loads typefaces from Google Fonts. A visitor's browser may connect to Google's font servers to retrieve those files. Google applies its own privacy terms to that request. A future version may self-host the fonts to reduce third-party requests.

5. External links

Articles may link to other websites. Those sites control their own content, cookies, analytics and privacy practices. Following an external link takes you outside this policy.

6. Advertising and affiliate links

The site does not currently run third-party display advertising. Some product links are paid affiliate links, which can allow a retailer or affiliate network to recognize that a visit or purchase followed a Beautiful Men's Club link. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Paid links are disclosed where they appear, and using one does not increase the reader's price.

7. Email retention and removal

Messages may be retained as long as reasonably useful for correspondence, editorial records, corrections or the founding-reader list. You may request removal from the contact list or deletion of ordinary correspondence by emailing the publication. Some records may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, legal compliance or documenting a correction.

8. Children

Beautiful Men's Club is written for a general adult audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child sent personal information may contact the publication to request its removal.

9. Security

No website or email system can promise absolute security. The publication aims to collect as little information as practical and to avoid pretending that a static lifestyle site needs to know everything about its readers.

10. Future accounts, payments or newsletter services

If the site later adds user accounts, payments, comments, a hosted newsletter platform or member tools, the relevant provider and data practices will be disclosed before or when those features launch. This policy will be revised to reflect the actual service rather than describing features that do not yet exist.

11. Changes to this policy

This page may be updated as the publication changes. The date at the top will show the latest revision. Material changes should be written plainly enough that a reasonable reader can understand what changed.

12. Contact

Privacy questions and removal requests may be sent to hello@beautifulmensclub.com.
